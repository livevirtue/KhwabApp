Privacy Policy: Khwab ki Tabeer (خوابوں کی تعبیر)

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

What the app downloads

The app downloads dream-interpretation entries, topics and contact details from our server (Supabase). These requests contain no personal information. Downloaded content is cached on your phone so the app works offline.

Favourites and language

Your saved symbols and language choice are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us.

Sending a dream on WhatsApp

If you use "Send your dream", the app opens WhatsApp with a message you wrote. The app itself does not store or send this message; WhatsApp sends it only when you press send. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+).
